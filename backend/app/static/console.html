        Session completeness —

        Read-only check of what has been captured. Surfaces anything missing while the participant is still here. Stops nothing — fix missing items (e.g. push a post-questionnaire from the Post stage), then re-check.

        Finish session

        Sessions are UNREPEATABLE. This gracefully stops recording (mp4 trailer written) and perception, re-checks completeness, and finalizes only on your explicit confirmation. Nothing is deleted; any failure is surfaced and the session is not silently completed.

        Read-only: writes the raw dumps + analysis frames + data dictionary to the exports directory.
